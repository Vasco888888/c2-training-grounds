Designed to mimic the official C2 Proficiency digital exam: the same parts, question types, numbering and marking, with an on-screen layout and timing modelled on the computer-based test.

## Features

- **Exam conditions**: the stopwatch starts by itself, and a test can't be paused, saved halfway or resumed. Leave it and the attempt is gone.
- **Exam mode**: a full Reading and Use of English paper (one random test from each of Parts 1–7) or a Writing paper (Parts 1 and 2), each under a 1 hour 30 minute countdown. Move freely between parts, with no pause and no feedback until you finish. The Reading paper is marked out of 72 and converted to an estimated score on the 180–230 scale with a grade (A 220–230, B 213–219, C 200–212, Level C1 180–199).
- **Instant scoring** with a full explanation for every question, showing why the answer is right and why the other options fail.
- **Official-style marking**: Part 4 answers are split into two parts worth one mark each, and Parts 4, 5 and 6 count double, so the paper totals 72 marks as in the real exam.
- **British English** throughout, with US and UK spellings both accepted in typed answers.
- **Resizable panels**: in Parts 5, 6 and 7 and in Writing, drag the divider between the text and the questions to give more room to either side.
- **Highlighting and notes** on the text while you work.
- **History** of every attempt, with a backup you can export and import.
- **Writing practice** with a word counter against the target range, and a ready-made prompt for getting your answer assessed.

## What's inside

| Part | Task | Tests |
|------|------|:-----:|
| 1 | Multiple-choice cloze | 8 |
| 2 | Open cloze | 8 |
| 3 | Word formation | 8 |
| 4 | Key word transformation | 8 |
| 5 | Multiple choice (long text) | 3 |
| 6 | Gapped text | 3 |
| 7 | Multiple matching | 3 |
| W1 | Writing: essay | 4 |
| W2 | Writing: article, letter, report or review | 4 |

All texts and questions are original and written at C2 level.

## Getting started

1. Download or clone the repository.
2. Open `index.html` in any modern browser.

Your results are stored in the browser (localStorage), so use **History → Export backup** now and then to keep a copy.

## Built with

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no build step and no dependencies.

Built by a C2 Proficiency student, for C2 Proficiency students.
